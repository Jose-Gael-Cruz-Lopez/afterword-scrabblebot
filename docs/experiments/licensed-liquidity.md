# Narrow liquidity experiment — not deployed

`licensed-liquidity.mts` compares a narrow seven-letter cashout exception with the existing policy in48fresh matched scenarios /96games, with24per auction mode and each seat4times/mode. Rosters contain licensed clones and mutants. The rule requires6auctions without a purchase, cash below the median of8recent positive paid prices, at most20cash, a7+letter word worth at least24, and at least8auctions left for rebuilding.

All48paired scores were identical; both arms had9wins, mean78.25. The rule did not demonstrate a useful changed outcome in this sample, so it is research only and is not imported by the live worker. Its two behavioral tests pass, but passing implementation tests is not evidence of strategic benefit. The report and runner retain all pairs/source hashes. The16615 public counterfactual in `licensed-live-diagnosis.md` remains a useful future joint-funding hypothesis rather than a validated production change.
